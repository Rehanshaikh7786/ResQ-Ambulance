SOSTAP TO REPORT

How it works

📸1 · Photo + GPSBystander sends a photo of the incident with live location.
🧠2 · Injury detectionAI vision finds bleeding, burns, hazards. Triage P1–P4 is calculated.
🚑3 · Nearest ambulanceClosest suitable unit is picked first and held in reserve.
🏥4 · Hospital alertedBest hospital by capability, beds, ICU, specialists and ETA gets the case + photo.
✅5 · Approve → dispatchOn approval the ambulance goes urgently. Decline or timeout → next hospital.
📍6 · Live trackingGoogle Maps tracking for the caller, control room, hospital and driver.
